03 · How content flows

Film once, publish across the month.

The heavy filming happens in week 1 and the rest of the month is fed from it. That only works if week 1 produces material the later weeks can cut from.

1

Week 1 · Shoot

YouTube batch and series shots. Mark the clip candidates in each video while scripting.

2

Week 2 · Series

Series gets finished and goes out Sunday. Short-form starts cutting.

3

Week 3 · Cut

Two short-form batches, pulled from the week 1 videos and the month's footage.

4

Week 4 · Series

Second series week, out on Sunday, plus one short-form batch. Then reset: pick next month's scenarios and shots before week 1.

04 · Feedback

An honest read.

The shape is right. These are the places it will strain.

What holds up
  • Client work first, every week. Content bends around it, not the other way.
  • One production week, two series weeks, one lighter week. The heavy effort is concentrated, not scattered.
  • Batching YouTube into 1–2 days protects the rest of the month.
1

Week 1 is carrying the month

Issue. A month of weekly multi-segment videos, filmed and edited in 1–2 days, plus series shots, plus a full client week. That is the highest-risk week by a distance.

Fix. Script every video before week 1 starts, so the days are only for filming and cutting. Consider splitting edits into week 1 and the start of week 2.

2

The vlog and the Thursday episode have no slot

Issue. Your cadence still includes a vlog every two weeks and the HM series on Thursdays. Neither appears in any week here.

Fix. Decide where they are filmed and edited. The simplest option is that the vlog and HM episodes come from the weekly b-roll, with a short edit inside weeks 2 to 4.

3

Week 3 is the only real buffer

Issue. With a series in weeks 2 and 4, a fixed Sunday deadline closes each of them. If client work grows, as you expect, week 3 is the only week where content can give.

Fix. Shoot the week 4 series material early, in week 3 or alongside week 1, so week 4 is mostly editing. Treat week 4's short-form batch as the first thing to drop.

4

Short-form content depends on week 1

Issue. If week 1 slips, the extractions in weeks 2 to 4 have nothing to be cut from.

Fix. Protect the week 1 batch above the series, and mark extraction candidates while filming, not during editing.

5

Client hours are not on the page yet

Issue. "Normal client work" is the biggest item and has no shape. It will expand to fill whatever is left.

Fix. Once you see a real week, block fixed client hours first and place content around them.
